1. Content of the app not under the EUPL, and other credits
-----------------------------------------------------------

Sounds (res/raw/connection_sound.wav, res/raw/disconnection_sound.wav)
  Generated with ElevenLabs (elevenlabs.io) on its free plan. They are NOT
  covered by the EUPL. ElevenLabs' terms for the free plan require the
  attribution "elevenlabs.io" and do not allow commercial use. Anyone who
  redistributes this app, or a work based on it, for a commercial purpose
  must remove or replace these two files.
  Sound effects: elevenlabs.io

WFAS v2 protocol
  The WiFi Audio Streaming wire protocol is this project's own. Its C
  reference implementation is published separately under the MIT License
  (Copyright (c) 2026 Marco Morosi, https://github.com/marcomorosi06/wfas-protocol)
  and is not part of this app.

AAC encoding uses Android's own MediaCodec API: the app bundles no
third-party media codec. It contains no proprietary dependency and no Google
Play Services or ML Kit component.
